ASSET THEO KENH
================

Moi kenh co 1 thu muc rieng. Renderer tu dong tim:
- logo.png / logo.webp / logo.jpg  -> watermark nhe, opacity/vi tri theo channels_config.json
- cover.jpg / cover.png / poster.jpg -> anh bia chen o dau video theo channels_config.json

Thu tu tim thu muc:
1. asset_folder khai bao trong channels_config.json
2. channel_assets/<TEN KENH DUNG NGUYEN VAN>/
3. channel_assets/<TEN_KENH_KHONG_DAU>/
4. channel_assets/default/

Vi du kenh "Vu Tru Phim":
channel_assets/Vũ Trụ Phim/logo.png
channel_assets/Vũ Trụ Phim/cover.jpg

Khong co file thi renderer tu bo qua, video van chay binh thuong.
